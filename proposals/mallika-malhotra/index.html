Step 02

What We Build

One Claude skill. Built to think like you, sound like you, and deliver like you. Every time, without you.

Here's exactly how the skill works. From the moment client answers land in front of you, to the moment they receive their branded report:

1
Guided intake: 10 to 15 fixed questions

The skill opens with a clear, warm briefing, sets expectations, explains what's coming. Then asks each question one by one, in your voice, with the right prompting so even non-writers give strong, usable answers. No drift. No "I don' t know how to answer this."

2
Framework processing: your full knowledge stack in the engine

All of your frameworks, knowledge docs, workbooks, and existing bots. Loaded as the intelligence layer. The skill processes every answer through your complete methodology. It doesn't approximate your thinking. It replicates it.

3
Input summary: reflecting back what they said

Before the full report, the skill generates a clean summary of client inputs. They feel heard. They see themselves reflected back clearly. This alone builds trust in everything that follows. Before they've read a single recommendation.

4
Branded HTML report, delivered and downloadable as PDF

The final output is a beautiful, on-brand HTML report. Personalized header. Snapshot section. Niche positioning options. Messaging direction. Actionable assets, Instagram bio, LinkedIn headline, they can use the same day. Fully downloadable as a PDF they can keep, share, and refer back to.

5
Your manual beta, now powered

Client fills your Google Form → you paste answers into the skill → it generates the full branded report → you deliver. Even in manual mode, prep time drops from 60 minutes to under 5. Every single time. When you're ready to automate, the hard part is already done.
