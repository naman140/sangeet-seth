Step 02

What is Care Companion

An AI-powered content guidance experience designed to replicate the expert’s recommendation pathway, completely rooted in their own published material.

Care Companion functions as a strategic content discovery system, guiding parents through a clear pathway using existing video resources and structured recommendations:

1
1. Content Selection & Ingestion

Up to 75 high-priority approved YouTube videos are selected by your team to represent the primary educational topics and themes.

2
2. Knowledge Layer Extraction

Accessible transcripts, captions, and source text from the selected videos are compiled to form the base knowledge layer for the system.

3
3. Semantic Ingestion & Indexing

The extracted text is processed, structured, and indexed, creating a semantic map of the expert's advice and content resources.

4
4. Guided Intake Flow

A parent arrives and answers a short, natural, and guided set of questions about the specific child-related concern they are experiencing.

5
5. Real-Time Index Matching

The system analyzes user responses in real-time, matching their concern against the structured semantic index of the approved library.

6
6. Personalized Video Recommendations

The parent is presented with a personalized roadmap recommending the most relevant existing videos and explaining why they match.

⚠️

Important Product Framing & Scope Safety

Care Companion is built strictly as a content discovery and personalization system, not an unlimited library ingestion archive and not a diagnostic medical AI tool. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or provide medical advice. Every output reinforces that Recommendations are strictly educational resources based on existing published materials, prompting parents to consult professionals for clinical concerns.
